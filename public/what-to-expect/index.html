Your first appointment

What to expect at your first tax appointment

Your first step with Your Tax Nerd in Dover, NJ is a free 30-minute discovery call by phone or video. Mike Frey learns about your situation, answers your questions and quotes your fee. Then you gather your documents, meet when it suits you, and review your return before it is filed.

Step by step

What happens from first call to filed return?

  1. Reach out

    Call or text (973) 202-3530, or send a message with the form. Tell us a little about what you need.

  2. Free 30-minute discovery call

    By phone or video. We talk through your year, answer your questions and quote your fee.

  3. Gather your documents

    We send a checklist for your situation. Share your forms through our mobile app or in person.

  4. Your appointment

    Online, by video, or face to face at your home, during the day, in the evening or on a weekend.

  5. Review and sign

    We go through your return line by line in plain words. Nothing is filed until you sign.

  6. Filed, with a copy for you

    We file your return and give you a copy to keep with your records.

Checklist

What should you bring to your tax appointment?

Not everyone needs everything on this list. Bring what applies to you, and we tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Last year's federal and New Jersey tax returns
  • A photo ID for you, and your spouse if you file together
  • Social Security numbers and birth dates for everyone on your return
  • W-2 forms from every job you had this year
  • 1099 forms for freelance, bank interest, dividends, retirement or app income
  • Form SSA-1099 if you received Social Security benefits
  • Form 1098 for mortgage interest, and your property tax bills
  • Your rent receipts or lease if you rent your home
  • Form 1098-T for college tuition and 1098-E for student loan interest
  • Child care costs and your provider's name, address and tax ID
  • Form 1095-A if you bought health insurance through the marketplace
  • Business income and expense records, and your mileage log
  • Records of any estimated tax payments you made
  • Your bank routing and account number for a direct deposit refund

Please do not send these documents through the contact form on this website. We tell you how to share them once you are a client.

Questions

Questions about your first appointment

What if I am missing a form?

Bring what you have. We tell you what is missing and how to get it. Many forms can be downloaded from your employer, your bank, or your online account with the IRS.

Can I meet at my home?

Yes. Face to face meetings at your home, or another place that suits you, are available. You can also meet online or by video.

Can my spouse join the appointment?

Yes. If you file together, it helps when both of you can join, online or in person.

How long will my return take?

It depends on how complete your documents are and how busy the season is. We give you an idea of timing on your free call.
