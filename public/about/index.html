The story

Why did Mike start Your Tax Nerd?

Mike started Your Tax Nerd so everyday people and small business owners could get professional tax help at a fair price.

Some people pay a lot for tax help and still do not understand their return. Others do it themselves and worry they missed something. Your Tax Nerd gives you one person who knows your situation, explains it in plain words, and is easy to reach when a question comes up.
