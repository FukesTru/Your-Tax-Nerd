Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping for small businesses in Dover, NJ

Your Tax Nerd keeps the books for small businesses and individuals in Dover, NJ and beyond. Bookkeeping means recording every dollar that comes in and goes out, so you know where your cash stands. Clean books also make tax time faster, because your numbers are ready when your return is due.

Signs it is time

How do you know you need a bookkeeper?

If any of these sound like your business, bookkeeping help pays off well before tax time.

  • You do not know if you made money last month.Your bank balance goes up and down, but you cannot see your profit.
  • Business and personal spending are mixed together.One card, one account, and no easy way to tell them apart.
  • Your receipts are in a pile.Or a drawer, a glove box and an inbox.
  • Tax time is a scramble every year.You spend days rebuilding the year before your return can start.
  • You want to grow.Hiring, borrowing or buying equipment is easier with numbers you trust.

What is included

What does bookkeeping with Your Tax Nerd include?

Accurate records of every transaction, kept in a way you can read.

Recording every transaction

Money in and money out, each one recorded and sorted into the right category, such as supplies, rent or sales.

Reconciling your accounts

Reconciling means checking your records against your bank and card statements line by line, so they match.

Reports you can read

A profit and loss report shows what you earned and spent. A balance sheet shows what you own and what you owe.

Ready for tax time

When your books are current, your tax return starts from clean numbers instead of a pile of paper.

Cash flow

Why does bookkeeping help your cash flow?

Cash flow is the money moving in and out of your business. When your books are current, you can see slow months coming, set money aside for taxes, and spot costs that keep creeping up. Decisions get easier when the numbers are in front of you.

Questions

Questions about bookkeeping

What is the difference between bookkeeping and tax preparation?

Bookkeeping records your income and costs through the year. Tax preparation uses those records to file your return. When the books are clean, the return is faster and easier to check.

Can you help if my books are months behind?

Yes. Tell us how far behind you are on your free call. We look at what you have and quote the work to bring your books up to date.

Do I need bookkeeping software?

Not to get started. If you already use bookkeeping software, tell us which one on your free call. If you do not, we can talk about what fits your business.

How often will you update my books?

That depends on how busy your business is. Some businesses need monthly bookkeeping, others are fine with less. We agree on a schedule on your free call.

Do you do bookkeeping for individuals?

Yes. Some people want help tracking household money, a rental property or a side business. Tell us what you need on your free call.
