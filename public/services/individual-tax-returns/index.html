Personal tax returns

Individual and family tax returns in Dover, NJ

Your Tax Nerd prepares federal and New Jersey income tax returns for individuals and families in Dover, NJ and beyond. Mike Frey goes through your documents, asks about changes in your year, and looks for the credits and deductions you qualify for. You review and sign before anything is filed.

Who it is for

Who comes to us for a personal tax return?

Anyone who wants their return done right, with someone to ask. These are the people we see most.

  • People with a W-2 jobA W-2 is the form your employer sends showing your pay and the tax already taken out.
  • Families with childrenChildren can bring credits for the child, for child care and for working families.
  • Parents of college studentsTuition can qualify for federal education credits. Bring the 1098-T form from the school.
  • Homeowners and rentersNew Jersey lets homeowners and renters claim a property tax deduction or credit on the state return.
  • RetireesSocial Security, pensions and IRA withdrawals each follow their own tax rules.
  • People with side incomeGig work, freelance jobs and rental income add forms. We make sure each one is reported correctly.

Credits and deductions

Which credits and deductions do we check for you?

A credit lowers your tax dollar for dollar. A deduction lowers the income your tax is figured on. Who qualifies depends on your income, your family and your filing status, so we check each one against your situation.

  • Child Tax Credit
  • Earned Income Tax Credit, federal and New Jersey
  • Credit for child and dependent care costs
  • American Opportunity and Lifetime Learning education credits
  • Student loan interest deduction
  • New Jersey property tax deduction or credit
  • New Jersey Child Tax Credit
  • Mortgage interest and charitable gifts, if you itemize

Questions

Questions about personal tax returns

When are personal tax returns due?

Federal and New Jersey individual income tax returns are usually due April 15. When April 15 falls on a weekend or a holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day.

What if I cannot file by the deadline?

You can request an extension, which gives you until October 15 to file. An extension gives you more time to file, but not more time to pay. Any tax you owe is still due in April, so call us before the deadline.

Do I have to come to an office?

No. You can work with us fully online through our mobile app, meet on Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp, or meet face to face at your home.

How long should I keep my tax records?

The IRS generally asks you to keep the records that support your return for at least 3 years after you file, and longer in some situations. Keep a copy of every return you file.

Can my spouse and I meet together?

Yes. Married couples who file together often meet together, online or in person, so both of you can ask questions and sign.
