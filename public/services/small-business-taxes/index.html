Small business taxes

Small business tax preparation in Dover, NJ

Your Tax Nerd prepares tax returns for self-employed people and small business owners in Dover, NJ and beyond. That includes Schedule C, the federal form for reporting self-employment income and expenses. Mike Frey sorts your income and costs, checks your 1099 forms, and walks you through the return before it is filed.

Who it is for

Who do we prepare business returns for?

People who work for themselves, whether it is full time or on the side.

  • Freelancers and contractorsYou get paid on 1099-NEC forms and nobody takes tax out of your pay.
  • Gig and delivery driversApp income, mileage and phone costs all belong on the return.
  • Tradespeople and service businessesTools, supplies, vehicles and insurance are part of the cost of doing business.
  • Shop and online sellersCard and marketplace payments arrive on 1099-K forms that need checking.
  • Single-member LLC ownersMost report business income on Schedule C with their personal return.

What we look at

What goes into a small business return?

We sort these before your return is prepared, so nothing is missed and nothing is counted twice.

Your income

Every 1099-NEC and 1099-K, plus cash, check and app payments, matched to your records.

Your business costs

Supplies, tools, software, advertising, insurance, phone and internet, sorted into the right categories.

Your vehicle and home office

Business miles and a home office that you use only for work can lower your taxable profit.

Self-employment tax

Social Security and Medicare on your profit, figured on Schedule SE and added to your income tax.

Estimated payments

What you paid during the year, and a plan for the quarterly payments ahead.

Your New Jersey return

Business profit also goes on your New Jersey return, which follows its own rules.

Records

What records should a small business keep?

Good records make your return faster and back up every number on it. Keep these through the year, and our bookkeeping service can keep them for you.

  • Every 1099 form you receive
  • Bank and credit card statements for the business
  • Receipts for business purchases
  • A log of business miles
  • Records of estimated tax payments
  • Last year's tax return

Questions

Questions about small business taxes

Do I have to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

Often, yes. When no tax is taken out of your pay, the IRS generally expects quarterly payments if you will owe $1,000 or more for the year. New Jersey has its own estimated payment rules. We work out the amounts with you.

When are quarterly estimated taxes due?

Usually April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15 of the next year. The dates move when they fall on a weekend or a holiday.

What is self-employment tax?

Self-employment tax is the Social Security and Medicare tax paid by people who work for themselves, because no employer pays a share. It is figured on your net business profit.

What is a 1099-K?

Form 1099-K reports payments you received through payment apps, online marketplaces and card processors. It can include personal payments by mistake, so we check it against your records before it goes on your return.

Does my business file its own return?

A business owned by one person, including most single-member LLCs, usually reports on Schedule C with the owner's personal return. Partnerships and corporations file their own returns. Ask on your free call which one fits your business.

Can I deduct my car?

If you use your car for business, part of the cost can usually be deducted, either by the mile or by actual costs. Either way, keep a log of your business miles.
