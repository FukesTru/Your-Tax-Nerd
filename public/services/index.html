Services

Tax and bookkeeping services in Dover, NJ

Your Tax Nerd in Dover, NJ offers 4 services for individuals, families and small businesses. Mike Frey prepares personal and small business tax returns, keeps the books for small businesses, and helps clients plan ahead to lower their tax legally. Every service starts with a free 30-minute discovery call.

What we do

Which services does Your Tax Nerd offer?

  • Individual and family tax returns

    Federal and New Jersey income tax returns for people with jobs, families, college students, retirement income or rental income. We look for every credit and deduction you qualify for, then go through the return with you before you sign.

    Personal returns

  • Small business tax preparation

    Returns for self-employed people, freelancers, contractors and small business owners. We sort your income and expenses, check your 1099 forms, and plan your quarterly estimated payments.

    Business returns

  • Bookkeeping

    We record and sort every transaction, match your accounts to your statements, and give you reports you can read. Your numbers stay current, so tax time goes faster.

    Bookkeeping

  • Tax planning and consulting

    Planning during the year, so April holds fewer surprises. We look at your withholding, estimated payments, retirement savings and business decisions, and find legal ways to lower your tax.

    Tax planning

Questions

Questions about our services

Which service should I start with?

Start with a free 30-minute discovery call. Tell us what is going on, and we tell you which service fits. Many small business owners use bookkeeping and tax preparation together.

Can I use more than one service?

Yes. Bookkeeping, tax planning and tax preparation work well together, because the same person knows your numbers all year.

Do you work with clients outside Dover?

Yes. We meet face to face with clients in Dover and nearby towns, and we work with clients anywhere online.
