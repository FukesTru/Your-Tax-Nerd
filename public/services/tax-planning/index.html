Tax planning and consulting

Tax planning for families and small businesses in Dover, NJ

Tax planning means making choices during the year that legally lower the tax you owe, instead of finding out in April. Your Tax Nerd works with individuals and small business owners in Dover, NJ and beyond to look ahead, plan estimated payments and understand cash flow, so tax time holds fewer surprises.

When it helps

When should you plan ahead for taxes?

Planning helps most in a year when something changes. These are the moments to call.

  • You start a business or a side job.You will need a plan for estimated payments and records from day one.
  • Your income goes up or down a lot.A raise, a bonus, a job loss or a big contract all change what you owe.
  • Your family changes.Marriage, a new baby or a child heading to college can change your credits.
  • You buy or sell a home.Mortgage interest, property taxes and a home sale each have tax rules.
  • You plan to retire or take money from a retirement account.When and how you withdraw can change the tax you pay.

What we cover

What does a tax planning conversation cover?

We focus on the choices that matter for your year.

  1. Your withholding

    Is enough tax coming out of your paycheck? We check your W-4 against what you will likely owe.

  2. Your estimated payments

    If you work for yourself, how much to pay each quarter, and when, for federal and New Jersey.

  3. Retirement savings

    How contributions to retirement accounts can lower the tax you owe now or later.

  4. Business decisions

    The tax side of buying equipment, hiring, or changing how your business is set up.

For business owners

How can planning help your business keep more of what it earns?

Profit, cash flow and taxes are connected. When you know your numbers during the year, you can set money aside for taxes, time big purchases, and avoid a surprise bill in April. Pair planning with our bookkeeping and your numbers stay current all year.

Questions

Questions about tax planning

When is the best time to plan?

Any time before the year ends, and as soon as something big changes. Many planning choices must be made by December 31 to count for that year.

What is a W-4 check-up?

A W-4 is the form that tells your employer how much tax to take out of each paycheck. A check-up compares that amount to what you will likely owe, so you do not end up with a big bill or an oversized refund.

Can planning lower my taxes?

Often it can, by timing income and costs, using retirement accounts, and claiming what you qualify for. Results depend on your situation, and we only use strategies that follow the tax law.

Is tax planning only for businesses?

No. Families, retirees and people with side income all benefit from looking ahead, especially in a year with big changes.
