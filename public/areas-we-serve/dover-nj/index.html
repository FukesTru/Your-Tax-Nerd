Dover businesses

Do you run a small business in Dover?

Dover has a busy downtown, with shops, restaurants and service businesses along Blackwell Street and the streets around it. Many are run by one owner who does the books at night.

We take that work off your plate, from bookkeeping to the business tax return.

  • Card and app paymentsWe check your 1099-K forms against your records, so personal payments are not taxed as sales.
  • Books that stay currentMonthly or quarterly bookkeeping, so you always know your profit.
  • Quarterly estimated taxesA plan for what to set aside and when to pay it, federal and New Jersey.
  • Your personal return tooMost small business owners report their business on their personal return, so we handle both together.
