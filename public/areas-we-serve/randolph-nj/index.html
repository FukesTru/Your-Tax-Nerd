Paying for college in Randolph? Check your education credits.

Randolph is home to County College of Morris, and many Randolph families have a student in college, at CCM or farther away.

Federal tax law offers 2 education credits, the American Opportunity Tax Credit and the Lifetime Learning Credit. Each has its own rules about who qualifies and which costs count.

  • Bring Form 1098-TThe school sends this form each year. It shows tuition billed or paid, and it is the starting point for any education credit.
  • Keep your receiptsRecords of what you paid for tuition and required course materials help support the credit.
  • Student loan interestYour lender sends Form 1098-E. The interest may be deductible, depending on your income.
  • Decide who claims the studentYour return and your student's return need to agree on who claims them as a dependent.

Questions

Questions from Randolph clients

Can I claim an education credit if my child goes to County College of Morris?

Possibly. Community college tuition can count toward the federal education credits, as long as you and your student meet the rules. We check this when we prepare your return.

What if my college student files their own return?

Their return and yours need to agree on who claims them as a dependent. We can prepare both, so the 2 returns line up.
