Denville commuters

Commute to New York from Denville? You likely file in 2 states.

Denville station is on NJ Transit's Morristown Line and Montclair-Boonton Line, and many Denville residents work in New York.

If you live in New Jersey and work in New York, New York taxes the wages you earn there. You file a New York nonresident return for that income and a New Jersey resident return for all of your income.

  • A credit for tax paid to New YorkNew Jersey gives you a credit for the tax you paid to New York on the same wages, so they are not fully taxed twice.
  • Days you worked from homeNew York has its own rules for nonresidents who work from home for a New York employer. Those days can still count as New York days.
  • Keep a work location recordA simple calendar of where you worked each day helps your returns line up.
  • Check your W-2Your W-2 should show wages and tax for each state. We check that both match your returns.

Questions

Questions from Denville clients

Do I have to file a New York return if I live in Denville?

If you earned wages working in New York, usually yes. You file a New York nonresident return for that income, plus your New Jersey resident return.

Will I pay tax twice on the same wages?

New Jersey gives you a credit for the tax you paid to New York on the same income, so the wages are not fully taxed twice. Getting the credit right takes care.

What about the days I worked from home?

New York has its own rules for nonresidents who work from home for a New York employer, and those days can count as New York days. Bring a record of where you worked.

Can Mike meet with me in Denville?

Yes. Face to face meetings at your home are available, including evenings and weekends. You can also meet online or by video.
