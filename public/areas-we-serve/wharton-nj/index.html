Raising kids in Wharton? These credits are worth checking.

A credit lowers your tax dollar for dollar. Who qualifies depends on your income, the age of each child and your filing status, so we check each one against your situation.

  • Earned Income Tax CreditFederal and New Jersey credits for working people with low to moderate income.
  • New Jersey Child Tax CreditA state credit for younger children, depending on your income.

Questions

Questions from Wharton clients

Can both parents claim the same child?

No. Only one return can claim a child for most credits. Separated or divorced parents follow special rules about who claims the child, so tell us about your situation.

What do I need for the child care credit?

The name, address and tax ID number of your child care provider, and how much you paid. Ask your daycare for a year-end statement.

I am filing my first tax return. Can you help?

Yes. We walk you through it step by step and explain what each form means.

Can Mike meet with us at home in Wharton?

Yes. Face to face meetings at your home are available, including evenings and weekends. You can also meet online or by video.
