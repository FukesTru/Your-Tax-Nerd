Questions

Questions people ask before they call

Straight answers about cost, documents and how to meet. More in our questions and answers.

How much does it cost to have Your Tax Nerd do my taxes?

Every return is different, so we quote your fee on the free 30-minute discovery call, before any work starts. The price depends on how many forms you have, whether you have business or rental income, and how many states you file in. See how pricing works.

Do I have to come to an office?

No. You can work with us fully online through our mobile app, meet on Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp, or meet face to face at your home or another place that suits you.

Can I meet in the evening or on a weekend?

Yes. Our regular hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and evening and weekend appointments are available. Ask for the time that works for you.

What documents do I need for my tax return?

Usually your W-2 and 1099 forms, last year's tax return, and records of costs like mortgage interest, property taxes, child care and college. After your free call, we send you a list for your situation. Here is the full first appointment checklist.

Do you work with self-employed people and small businesses?

Yes. We prepare returns for freelancers, contractors and small business owners, keep their books, and help plan quarterly estimated taxes. Read about small business tax preparation.

Do you only work with people in New Jersey?

No. Our address is in Dover, NJ, and we work with clients anywhere online. If you lived or worked in more than one state this year, tell us on your free call.
