What changes the price

What makes one tax return cost more than another?

The more a return involves, the more time it takes. These are the things that change a quote.

  • The number of income formsOne W-2 is simpler than several W-2s, 1099s and investment statements.
  • Self-employment or business incomeA business adds Schedule C, expenses and self-employment tax.
  • Rental propertyRent, repairs and depreciation each need their own reporting.
  • Investments you soldStock, fund and crypto sales are reported one by one.
  • More than one stateLiving or working in a second state means a second state return.
  • How organized your records areSorted records take less time than a box of loose receipts.
